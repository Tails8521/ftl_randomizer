A ship must be equiped to have a reasonable chance against enemies from sector one and two, this can come from either weapons or boarding
To prevent weapons loadouts that cannot get trough the one shield layer most enemies in sector one and two, weapons are considered "enough" according to if they meet at least one criteria from the list. The list of criteria depends of their type:
- Beam:
    - Deals at least 2 damage
    - Deals at least 1 damage and there is at least one other ion or laser weapon present on the ship
- Bombs: never enough
- Laser:
    - Has more than one shot
    - There is at least one other ion or laser weapon present on the ship
    - Has shield piercing of at least one
- Missiles:
    - Deals at least 2 damage and there is at least 28 starting missiles on the ship (14 starting missiles if the explosive replicator augment is present on the ship)


For boarding, the ship has to start with the teleporter system present at least one of the following:
- 3 crew including at least 2 average/strong fighters or 1 strong fighter
- 2 crew including at least 1 average/strong fighter and the ship has a boarding drone with at least 10 starting drone parts

Boarding races:
weak: engi, zoltan
average: human, slug, lanius
strong: rock, crystal, mantis

Combat and beam drones are viable if the ship has an ion weapon (still need to evaluate the viability of ion1/stunner/chain tho)
